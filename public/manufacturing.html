BEHIND THE SCENES

From first cut to final check.

Our people are at the heart of every garment. The work moves through careful preparation, skilled sewing, considered finishing, inspection and packing.

  1. 01

    Fabric cutting

    Fabrics are prepared, measured and cut to garment specifications.

  2. 02

    Sewing

    Tailors and sewing-machine operators bring the pieces together.

  3. 03

    Finishing

    Buttons, zips, labels and garment details receive their final touches.

  4. 04

    Quality control

    Workmanship, sizing and finishing are inspected before dispatch.

  5. 05

    Packaging

    Finished garments are organised, folded and prepared for delivery.
