Overview

Customers can’t contact you yet. Add your WhatsApp number, phone or email in so the contact buttons show on the store.

Getting started

  1. Add your contact details and currency under Settings.
  2. Create products with a price, sizes and stock, then upload photos. The first photo is the cover.
  3. Leave a product as a draft to hide it from the store until it's ready.
  4. Customer orders arrive under Orders, marked awaiting payment until payments go live.
